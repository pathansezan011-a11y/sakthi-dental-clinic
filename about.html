Dr. Anupriya founded Sakthi Dental Clinic in Hosur with a clear vision: to establish an empathetic, patient-centric dental practice where families receive unhurried, gentle, and transparent oral healthcare.

Our Guiding Principles

The Values That Guide Our Practice

Every clinical decision, patient interaction, and protocol at Sakthi Dental Clinic is driven by our core tenets of medical integrity and patient comfort.

Patient-Centered Empathy

We listen actively to your dental concerns, past anxieties, and personal health goals. We never rush appointments, ensuring you feel completely supported from start to finish.

Conservative Dentistry

Our primary goal is always to protect and preserve your natural tooth structure. We advocate minimally invasive treatments that sustain long-term oral vitality.

Transparent Clinical Advice

We provide straightforward recommendations without unnecessary procedures. You receive clear explanations of what is needed, why it is needed, and what to expect.

Safety & Infection Control

Strict 4-Step Sterilization Protocol

Your safety is our absolute priority. We adhere to rigorous sanitization standards for every single instrument and treatment surface.

01

Decontamination

Immediate chemical disinfection of all reusable instruments in medical enzymatic solutions directly after each procedure.

02

Ultrasonic Cleaning

High-frequency ultrasonic baths eliminate microscopic debris from instrument grooves and fine working edges.

03

Hermetic Pouches

Instruments are sealed in medical-grade sterilization pouches with chemical color indicators verifying complete sterility.

04

Class-B Autoclaving

Pressurized high-temperature steam autoclaving destroys all bacterial, viral, and fungal organisms before individual unwrapping in front of you.
