Privacy Policy

Our commitment to protecting your personal information and maintaining strict clinical healthcare confidentiality.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Sakthi Dental Clinic ("we", "our", or "the Clinic"), situated at B2/8, SBM Layout, Anthivadi, Hosur, Tamil Nadu 635109, is committed to upholding strict ethical and legal data protection standards. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information collected when you browse our website, make appointment inquiries, or communicate with our clinical reception.

2. Information We Collect

When using our website, you may voluntarily submit personal details through our appointment and inquiry forms:

  • Full Name
  • Phone / Mobile Number
  • Email Address
  • Treatment Preferences (e.g., Teeth Cleaning, Root Canal, Aligners)
  • Preferred Appointment Date and Time Slot
  • Optional clinical messages or symptoms voluntarily provided

3. Purpose of Information Collection

Information collected through our website is used strictly for legitimate healthcare and clinic administration purposes:

  • Confirming, rescheduling, or managing dental consultation bookings
  • Communicating with you via phone, SMS, email, or WhatsApp regarding your inquiries
  • Preparing our clinical team with relevant background for your upcoming visit
  • Responding to patient questions regarding our services and clinic location in Hosur

4. Absolute Non-Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal contact information or health details to third parties or advertising brokers under any circumstances. Data submitted is solely accessed by authorized clinic personnel responsible for appointment management and direct patient care.

5. Medical Confidentiality & Records

Clinical evaluations, dental charts, radiographs, and diagnoses conducted at Sakthi Dental Clinic are subject to professional medical confidentiality in accordance with the Dental Council of India ethics and applicable Indian healthcare regulations. Medical information is kept strictly private between the patient and Dr. Anupriya.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical, operational, and physical security safeguards to protect patient inquiries from unauthorized access, accidental alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request verification of the personal contact details we hold on record
  • Request corrections or updates to your contact information
  • Request deletion of your non-clinical inquiry records from our communication logs
  • Opt out of future appointment reminders or clinic communications at any time

8. Contacting Us for Privacy Inquiries

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact our clinic administration:
